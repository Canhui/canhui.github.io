Canhui Wang is currently a Lecturer (Research & Teaching) with Network and Information Security, the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Huaqiao University, Xiamen. He is also affiliated with Xiamen Key Laboratory of Data Security and Blockchain Technology, and Key Laboratory of Computer Vision and Machine Learning of Fujian Provincial Universities. Before that, he was a senior engineer with a full sponsorship by Research Talent Hub, Innovation and Technology Fund (RTH-ITF), with Applied Cryptosystems, Trust and AI technologies, at the ASTRI Security Lab, Hong Kong Applied Science and Technology Research Institute (ASTRI), Hong Kong, under the direction of [Dr. Eric P. F. Lam](https://www.linkedin.com/in/peifunglam2/). He received his Ph.D. degree in computer science from Hong Kong Baptist University, Hong Kong, under the supervision of [Prof. Xiaowen Chu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/xiaowen-chu-477b9120/), in 2023; and B.Eng. degree in software engineering from Shandong University, Jinan, China, in 2016. He is a member of IEEE and a member of CCF. His research area is networking, security, and distributed systems, including network measurement, performance evaluation, and blockchain systems.
